What it does

Everything from photo to sale

Built for people who move real volume — organized inventory, accurate identity, and clean marketplace listings without the manual data entry.

📷

AI card scanning

Photograph a card and SSAI recognizes it and matches it to its exact catalog identity — set, number, rarity, and finish.

🗂️

Smart inventory

Every card becomes a structured record with condition, quantity, and cost, searchable and organized across your collection.

📈

Live price tracking

Track current market value per card and per condition, and watch how your inventory's value moves over time.

🏷️

Marketplace export

Generate clean, valid listings for major marketplaces so cards go from shelf to sale without hand-typing every field.

🎴

Multiple games

One app for more than one trading-card game, with accurate per-game catalog data and imagery.

☁️

Secure cloud sync

Optional account-based backup keeps your collection safe and synced across devices — the app works fully offline too.
